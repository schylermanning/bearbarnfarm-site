Bear Barn Farm Privacy Policy

Last updated November 23, 2026

Bear Barn Farm is a small family shop in Indiana. We sell digital Frame TV art and printable wall art in our Etsy shop, etsy.com/shop/BearBarnFarm, and share our designs on Pinterest.

What this covers

This policy covers our shop and the private tool we use to post Pins to our own Pinterest account, the Bear Barn Farm Pin Scheduler.

Information we collect

  • Orders. When you buy from us on Etsy, Etsy processes your payment. It shares with us only what we need to fill the order, such as your name and Etsy username. Etsy's own privacy policy covers that data.
  • Pinterest. Our tool connects only to the Bear Barn Farm Pinterest account. It creates and manages our own Pins and boards and reads basic analytics for our own account. It does not collect, store or use any data about other Pinterest users.

How we use information

We use order information only to deliver your purchase and answer your questions. We never sell, rent or share personal information, and we don't use it for advertising.

Storage and security

Access keys for our Pinterest account are kept in an encrypted password manager. Order details stay in Etsy.

Your choices

To ask about, correct or delete any information we hold, message us through our Etsy shop. We reply within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Message Bear Barn Farm through our Etsy shop.
